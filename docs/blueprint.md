# **App Name**: RakshaTag Landing

## Core Features:

- Information Display: Display information about Raksha Tag, including its mission, how it works, and its benefits.
- Registration Link: Provide a clear call-to-action for users to register for the service, with a link to the registration page.
- Transparency Information: Display information about the organization's commitment to financial transparency, including links to monthly financial reports and archived reports.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: White or light gray for a clean and trustworthy feel.
- Secondary color: A calming blue (#3498db) to convey safety and reliability.
- Accent: Green (#2ecc71) to highlight positive actions and calls to action.
- Use a clear and simple layout with distinct sections for information, registration, and transparency.
- Employ recognizable icons for safety, security, and emergency to quickly communicate key features.

## Original User Request:
Raksha Tag
QR-Based Emergency Safety & Contact System

About Raksha Tag
Raksha Tag is a public service initiative to help people stay safe in emergency situations. Our QR-based ID tags link to emergency contact details, helping good samaritans or authorities contact family instantly.

How It Works
Each Raksha Tag carries a unique QR code. When scanned, it displays pre-linked emergency contact information of the person it belongs to. Useful for children, senior citizens, travelers, and vehicle owners.

Support the Cause
Raksha Tag is a service. We invite well-wishers to support us in expanding this initiative.

Contact Us
Email: admin@rakshatag.com

Project Report: Raksha Tag
Objective:
To provide a simple, accessible safety system that enables quick identification and communication in case of emergencies, through a unique QR code and a 10-digit ID system linked to personal and emergency information.
Step 1: User Registration Process
Platform: Mobile App / Website

Process:
1. User logs in using their mobile number.
2. OTP verification for authentication.
3. User fills in personal details:
   - Full Name
   - Date of Birth
   - Occupation
   - Residential Address
   - Blood Group
   - 5 Emergency Contact Numbers
4. After submitting the form, user is redirected to payment gateway.
5. Upon successful payment:
   - A unique 10-digit ID and QR Code is generated.
   - Two QR code stickers are printed and sent via postal service to the registered address.
   - Users can stick these on vehicles, school bags, helmets, etc.
Step 2: Promotion & Referral System
Registered users can refer others during registration by entering the referral ID of the person who introduced them.
When a new user registers using a referral, a reward/commission is credited to the referring user as a token of appreciation for spreading awareness and contributing to a social cause.
Step 3: Emergency Response Mechanism
In case of an emergency:
- Anyone can scan the QR code or call customer care and provide the 10-digit ID.
- Our system:
  - Identifies the registered person.
  - Immediately sends alerts to the 5 emergency contacts via call/SMS.
  - Customer care team will assist or coordinate further actions based on the situation.
- Once the situation is handled, the task is closed in the system.
Step 4: Funding & Financial Management
Sources of Income:
- User Registration Fees
- Voluntary Donations from individuals or organizations

Fund Management Policy:
- All funds are deposited into the official Raksha Tag Organization Account.
- These main funds are not used directly for operational costs.
- Instead, funds are invested in safe, government-approved schemes (e.g., Fixed Deposits).
- Only the interest/income generated from these investments is used for:
  - Employee salaries (Customer support, tech team)
  - App and website maintenance
  - Office infrastructure (rent, furniture, electricity)
  - Organization’s operational needs

Transparency & Public Disclosure:
🔹 Every individual who donates or supports financially, their name (with consent), contribution amount, and purpose will be recorded and transparently displayed.
🔹 The organization commits to monthly financial updates on the official website: www.rakshatag.com
🔹 These updates will include:
  - Total funds received (registration + donations)
  - Monthly expenditure report
  - Current investment details
  - Employee payment summary
  - Project progress report

🔒 Transparency is the backbone of Raksha Tag.
📅 Every 1st week of the month, financial and operational reports will be made public via the website.
📂 Archived monthly reports will also be available for review.
