# Raksha Tag - Landing Page

Welcome to the official landing page of **Raksha Tag** — a simple, accessible safety system that empowers fast identification and communication during emergencies through a unique **QR Code** and **10-digit ID system**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Instant Registration:** Mobile Number login with OTP authentication.
- **Personal Safety Profile:** Store vital personal and emergency contact information.
- **Unique Identification:** Receive a unique 10-digit ID and a scannable QR Code.
- **Emergency Assistance:** Immediate notifications to 5 emergency contacts via call/SMS.
- **Referral Rewards:** Refer friends and earn rewards for contributing to a social cause.
- **Financial Transparency:** Monthly public reports for complete fund management transparency.

---

## 📲 How It Works

1. **Register** on the app or website using your mobile number.
2. **Verify** via OTP and fill out your personal and emergency details.
3. **Pay** the registration fee securely.
4. **Receive** two printed QR code stickers via post.
5. **Stick** your QR codes on vehicles, helmets, school bags, etc.
6. **In an Emergency,** anyone can scan or call customer care to notify your emergency contacts instantly.
